Welcome

A Different Way to See Asia

Double Happiness is a travel blog and photo gallery dedicated to sharing authentic cultural experiences across Asia. The name comes from the Chinese character 囍 (Shuāngxǐ), meaning double happiness — a symbol that represents a fundamentally different way of thinking about joy and meaning.

Based in Macau for nearly a decade, I was fortunate to travel the continent in a relaxed, semi-local way — spending time absorbing cultures and the daily rhythms of local life rather than rushing between landmarks. This blog is about going deeper: the hidden temples, street scenes, local cuisine, and quiet moments that rarely make the travel guides.

Travel must always be with respect for local culture, and at the same time social and environmental well-being.
