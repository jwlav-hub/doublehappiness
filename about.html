A Different Way to See Asia

Double Happiness is a travel platform dedicated to sharing authentic cultural experiences across Asia. The name comes from the Chinese character 囍 (Shuāngxǐ), meaning double happiness — a symbol that represents a fundamentally different way of thinking about joy and meaning.

Based in Macau for nearly a decade, I was fortunate to travel the continent in a relaxed, semi-local way — spending time absorbing cultures and the daily rhythms of local life rather than rushing between landmarks. Here you'll find personal journal entries, practical travel guides, and AI-powered tools to help you plan smarter trips.

Welcome to Double Happiness

When I arrived in Macau SAR, China, I visited a Shanghai Tang retail store and noticed a prominent design on certain branded items with Chinese characters and asked what it meant. The translation for the characters is Shuāngxǐ — meaning Double Happiness — which ultimately became the title of this blog.

To me the translation presented a different way of thinking. Happiness just isn't something that doubles. In the Western world it really isn't quantifiable. To this end, I found it interesting as a step to understanding how these very different languages represent a different way of thinking — or even logic.

A Semi-Local Approach to Asia

Based in Macau for nearly 10 years, I have been fortunate to travel Asia in a casual, relaxed and semi-local modus operandi. Macau is a less than 5-hour flight to a dozen different countries and cultures. With this luxury of not having to rush like an international tourist on a once-in-a-lifetime vacation, I was fortunate to spend time absorbing cultures and the way of the locals.

The intent of this blog is to share experiences that go beyond designated tourist attractions and communicate a deeper cultural experience. In a divided world, travel is much more important than reaching a destination and taking a selfie. Travel should be about learning and understanding diversity in cultures.

Travel must always be with respect for local culture and at the same time social and environmental well-being.
